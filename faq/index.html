Orange County happy hour FAQ

What time is happy hour in Orange County?

3–6 PM is the classic Orange County window. Of the 62 spots on Cheers OC, 29 start at 3 PM and 35 end at 6 PM, and 20 run exactly 3–6 PM. A few start earlier, like 1 PM at The Sandpiper Lounge in Laguna Beach, and some run until 7 PM. 17 spots list a happy hour every day of the week, and the rest are mostly weekday-only.

Where are the best happy hours in Newport Beach?

Spots we track in Newport Beach include Fly-N-Fish Oyster Bar (Mon–Fri 3–6 PM, half dozen oysters for $14), The Cannery's Jellyfish Lounge (daily 3:30–6 PM, 40% off marked menu items), RED O (daily 3–6 PM and 9 PM–close) and Bayside Restaurant (daily until 7 PM, $10 select-spirit cocktails). All 10 Newport spots are in the Newport Beach section under the map.

Where are the best happy hours in Huntington Beach?

Spots we track in Huntington Beach include Killarney's Irish Pub and Baja Sharkeez (both Mon–Fri 3–7 PM, with 32oz double drinks), No Ka Oi (daily 3–7 PM, $7 fish tacos, mai tais and more), Treehouse on PCH, a rooftop at the Pasea Hotel (Mon–Fri 4–6 PM), and Sandbar Cocina y Tequila (Mon–Fri 3–6:30 PM). See all 7 in the Huntington Beach section.

Where can I find happy hour in Irvine and Costa Mesa?

We track 7 spots in Irvine and 6 in Costa Mesa. In Irvine, Eureka! (daily 3–6 PM and 9 PM–close) and CUCINA enoteca (daily 3–6 PM) run every day. In Costa Mesa, Descanso offers $5 beers and $10 taco skillets Mon–Fri 3–6 PM, Ospi has a daily 3–5:30 PM aperitivo menu, and Cafe Sevilla takes 50% off tapas Wed–Sun 5–7 PM.

Are there weekend happy hours in Orange County?

Yes. 23 of the 62 spots we track list a Saturday or Sunday happy hour, and 17 run every day. Newport Beach has the most weekend options (5), followed by Laguna Beach and Irvine (4 each). Mozambique in Laguna Beach runs Sat–Sun 2–4 PM, and Hook & Anchor in Dana Point runs Sat–Sun 3–5 PM. Use the day filter to see weekend deals.

Are there late-night or reverse happy hours in Orange County?

Yes, a handful. RED O in Newport Beach and Eureka! Irvine both run a second happy hour from 9 PM to close. Yard House Irvine Spectrum lists a late night from 10 PM to midnight, Yard House Costa Mesa lists 10 PM to close Sun–Wed, and Mickey's Irish Pub in Fullerton runs 8 PM to close Sun–Wed. Late windows change, so confirm with the venue.

Which Orange County cities have the most happy hour food deals?

Newport Beach leads with 7 spots offering food deals, followed by Huntington Beach and Costa Mesa with 6 each, Irvine with 5 and San Clemente with 4. In total, 42 of the 62 spots we track include food, from oysters and tacos to discounted small plates. Pick "Food" in the deal filter to show only those spots, or open a city in the list under the map.
